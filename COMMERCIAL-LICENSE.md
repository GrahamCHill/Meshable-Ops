# Commercial, proprietary and extra-branding exceptions

The [Meshable Ops Community License](LICENSE) allows community modifications and forks under strong same-license and source-sharing conditions, plus business use, hosting, easy installers, setup, deployment management, maintenance and paid community plugins. Hosting providers and service companies may charge for infrastructure and these services, including a profit margin. AWS, GCP, Azure and other provider bills do not breach the license. Providers may use their own infrastructure or a customer's infrastructure.

**A separate written agreement is required for application paywalls, closed-source offerings or branding/white-label changes beyond exposed customization features.** Community source changes, new features, bundled-plugin changes and forks do not need individual approval if they obey the license. They must provide complete covered source to recipients and network users under the same terms. Publishing only a base/community edition while withholding covered premium features is insufficient, including when those features are packaged as plugins or services.

Easy installers may change deployment scripts, manifests and configuration as reasonably necessary to deploy the application. Other changes must independently satisfy the community modification/source rules or an express written exception. Built-in branding settings remain allowed without an agreement.

Agreements must come from Graham Hill as original creator, a lawful rights-holding successor, or a current [Licensing Maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md) expressly authorized by that rights holder. A fork maintainer cannot appoint themselves or grant rights they do not hold. Support payments, plugin purchases, donations and contributions do not automatically supply permission.

An agreement may define covered code/versions/entities, paid application access, applicable licensing conditions, source-sharing exceptions, extra branding, development/maintenance contributions, fees and support. Its agreed terms may replace or supplement the corresponding community conditions for the identified covered works and derivative versions, to the extent of the granting party's authority. **Exceptions must be express:** permission to charge for application access does not waive source-sharing duties, and extra-branding approval does not authorize closed-source releases. A proprietary/premium arrangement may expressly permit covered source to remain private for the identified versions, rights and uses. The repository supplies no such agreement. Paid community plugins remain allowed under LICENSE section 4, alongside permitted hosting/services, but their covered source must remain available under the community terms unless a valid agreement expressly provides otherwise.

An agreement only covers rights the granting party owns or may sublicense. LICENSE section 3.1 requires fork changes to grant the project licensor permanent, royalty-free reuse and sublicensing rights, including commercial/proprietary releases, and source delivery on written request. This condition applies without a separate PR or CLA signature. An exception to the grant-back itself must also be express. Independent plugins and other code outside that grant still need adequate contributor or third-party permission before proprietary relicensing. A fork maintainer cannot waive inherited community terms for the original application or other contributors' code.

Contact [GrahamCHill](https://github.com/GrahamCHill). Preserve the agreement and verify licensing authority. Upstream components keep their own terms, and no application agreement waives those obligations. Existing valid licenses are not retroactively revoked.

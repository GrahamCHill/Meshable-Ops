It **does not authorize commercial distribution, sale/licensing of the application or forks, or commercial hosted/managed/SaaS offerings**.

Those offerings require an executed written license from Graham Hill as original creator, a lawful rights-holding successor, or a current [Licensing Maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md) expressly authorized by that rights holder. A fork maintainer cannot appoint themselves or grant rights they do not hold. Support payments, plugin purchases, donations and contributions do not automatically supply permission.

An agreement may define covered code/versions/entities, distribution/hosting rights, proprietary modifications, development/maintenance contributions, fees, support and premium functionality. None is supplied here. Paid community plugins remain allowed under LICENSE section 4; selling or commercially hosting the application alongside them still requires permission.
